## Data Observation

With the environment ready, this chapter verifies observability data and configures platform features through hands-on operations:

1. Generate business traffic and verify RUM, end-to-end tracing, logs, and Profiling.
2. Create a log Pipeline and verify structured fields.
3. Create monitors and SLOs, and import the product service operational quality dashboard.
