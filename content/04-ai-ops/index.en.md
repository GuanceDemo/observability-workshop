## AI-Driven Operations

This chapter builds on the anomalies and alerts generated in the previous chapter to demonstrate Guance AI capabilities:

1. Deploy Agent Runtime on an EKS node, use Obsy AI and Agent Teams to analyze Traces and alerts, and complete the fault handling loop.
2. Change the Agent to the `CVE Gap Analysis and Evidence Attribution Agent`, enable a read-only CVE scanning Skill, and use a daily Automation Task to perform gap analysis and evidence attribution from scan results, SBOMs, component versions, and official security advisories.
